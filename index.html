Bill and Peg Roberts started the company in Rockville, Maryland, in 1966. Their son Bob ran it next. Their grandson Will runs it today, the third generation of the family in the business. Along the way, Roberts grew to 57 locations across nine states, giving customers access to local Roberts locations throughout its service area.
